# Vision

## Mission
LinkedOut exists to make the hiring process more honest, transparent, and human. The platform flips the traditional dynamic so that companies compete for talent rather than talent competing for roles.

## Product Principles
- Transparency first
- Trust through evidence
- Fairness and accountability
- Respect for professional autonomy
- High signal, low noise

## Goals
1. Create a trusted professional network for evaluating companies.
2. Give employees a voice in assessing employer quality.
3. Help companies improve recruiting quality and employer brand.
4. Provide a scalable foundation for future AI-assisted decision support.

## Non-Goals
- Replacing all recruiting workflows in one release
- Supporting every employer type in the first phase
- Building a fully generalized job board without quality signals
